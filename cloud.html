What Zane does

Zane is an all-in-one fitness app for adaptive training, nutrition, health metrics, friends and coaching. It helps people plan workouts, log every set, track food and recovery, and adjust future training using the information from completed sessions.

The app runs in a browser and can be installed on a phone. Login is only needed to use the private app; this page is public so anyone can understand the product before signing in.

Adaptive training

Create plans, run workouts, record sets, use rest timers and review progress over time.

Nutrition and health

Log meals, recipes, calories, macros, water, steps, cardio and other daily health metrics.

Coaching

Clients can submit structured weekly check-ins to their coach, including optional progress photos.

Friends and sharing

Connect with friends, share selected progress and exchange lightweight workout feedback.

Optional Google Drive archive

Coaches may connect their own Google account to archive client check-ins. Zane creates a Google Sheet in the coach's Google Drive for each check-in and can include the check-in answers, summary metrics and optional photos when the coach enables that setting.

Zane requests only the limited Google Drive drive.file permission. This lets Zane work with files created by Zane for the archive; it does not read unrelated files in the coach's Drive. Disconnecting Google Drive stops future exports and removes Zane's stored access token.

Privacy and data

Zane uses account, training, nutrition, health and coaching data only to provide these features. Google Drive access is optional and is used only for the archive described above.
